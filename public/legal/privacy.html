1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Veda ("Veda", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by [Legal Entity Name], collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Veda mobile and web application (the "Service").

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: when you create an account, we collect your email address, full name, and (if you sign in with Google) your Google profile name and avatar image.

Profile & Preferences: academic stage, field of study, target GPA, currency, prayer calculation method, theme, and other preferences you choose to set.

Location Data: with your permission, we collect approximate or precise device location to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. You can decline location access.

User Content: tasks, notes, journal entries, study documents you upload, flashcards, habit and goal tracking data, prayer and Quran reading logs, and Ask Veda AI chat messages.

Financial Data: expense entries, budget figures, and savings goals you manually enter. We never collect or store bank/card numbers — payments are handled entirely by PayPal.

Subscription & Billing Status: trial start date, subscription tier, billing cycle, and status, received from PayPal to manage your Premium access.

Usage & Device Data: general technical information such as app version, device type, and crash/error logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

To operate the Service; calculate prayer times/Qibla direction; generate AI-assisted study plans, summaries, flashcards, financial insights, and chat responses; process and manage your subscription/trial; sync your data across devices; personalize your experience; and maintain security. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. AI Features & Third-Party AI Processing

Veda's AI features are powered by Google's Gemini AI models. Content you submit to these features (a question, an uploaded document, or relevant task/financial data) is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing, subject to Google's own API data handling terms. Do not submit sensitive information (government ID numbers, medical information, full financial account details) into AI features. AI responses may be inaccurate — see our Terms of Service for full disclaimers.

5. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with: Supabase (database, authentication, cloud storage, protected by row-level security); Google (Gemini API processing and Google Sign-In); and PayPal (subscription payment processing — Veda never sees your full card/bank details). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Veda or others.

Your data is stored in Supabase-hosted infrastructure behind row-level security restricting access to your own account, with data encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. A local device cache may also exist for performance, but is never the authoritative copy.
